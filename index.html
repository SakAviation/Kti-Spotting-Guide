Spotting Spots & Reference Photos

Aircraft photos by Sak. More shots in the Library →

Spot A

South Approach Road

Air Cambodia — Airbus A320 (XU-762)

Under the Runway 05 final approach path. Good angle for arrivals low over the perimeter.

Best time: Morning (06:30–11:30)
Lens: 70–200mm
Spot B

East Perimeter Bank

Cathay Pacific — Airbus A321neo (B-HPE)

On the perimeter fence line. Clear views of taxiing aircraft and line-up action.

Best time: Afternoon (14:30–17:30)
Lens: 100–400mm
Spot C

Main Terminal Deck

Etihad Airways — Airbus A321LR (A6-LRF)

Terminal glass view over the gate aprons, ground operations and close-up pushbacks.

Best time: Midday / all day
Lens: 24–105mm
Spot D

North Approach Boulevard

Vietnam Airlines (SkyTeam) — A321 (VN-A327)

Arrivals lined up for a Runway 23 touchdown against a clear sky.

Best time: Late afternoon / sunset
Lens: 70–300mm

Airport Info & Tips

Codes
IATA KTI · ICAO VDTI
Opened
9 September 2025, replacing Phnom Penh International (PNH)
Location
Kandal Province, south of Phnom Penh (sources say roughly 20–30 km)
Runway
One runway, 05/23, 4,000 m long and 60 m wide, with CAT II approach lighting at both ends
Taxiways
Two parallel taxiways (A and B) with rapid exits
Terminal
Main terminal with a curved pier extending from its northern end. A second pier and more runways are planned in later phases.
Magnetic variation
About 0.9° W
Photography
All aircraft photos by Sak

Spotter tips

  • Check the wind: the active runway flips with the wind, so the best spot changes on the day.
  • Light: keep the sun behind you.
  • Stay legal and safe: public areas only, follow signs and staff instructions, keep away from fences and restricted zones.
  • Be considerate: don’t block roads or driveways, and take your rubbish.
  • Heat and rain: bring water, sun protection and a rain cover.
  • Log your sightings: registration, airline, time and spot.
